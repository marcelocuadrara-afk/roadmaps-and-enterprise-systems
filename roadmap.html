Crawl rationale

Crawl establishes authority over data, controlled data movement, and an agreed credit workflow. These capabilities can be designed and built in parallel because none requires another to be complete before work begins. Completion means acceptance of the initial reporting and North American credit scope within twelve months, rather than enterprise replacement. Walk starts only when the relevant owners accept the rules, feeds and process controls. Without these gates, reporting automation would reproduce unreliable inputs, while loan automation would accelerate inconsistent handoffs and unresolved exceptions.

Q1-Q2 · Crawl

I1 Data governance redesign

Scope and capability: Approved owners, quality rules, access policy and escalation for selected reporting and credit fields.

Dependency: No completed Crawl prerequisite. Engineering and process design proceed concurrently; integrated Q4 acceptance aligns the outputs.

Acceptance: 100% of scoped critical data elements have owners and quality rules; no critical defect is unowned.

Reported activity: 2024 governance reset. Evidence: S1 printed p. 11.
Q1-Q4 · Crawl

I2 Strategic data platform onboarding

Dependency: No completed Crawl prerequisite. Engineering and process design proceed concurrently; integrated Q4 acceptance aligns the outputs.

Acceptance: 100% lineage for scoped critical fields; at least 99% approved data checks pass for two cycles; no unresolved critical defect.

Reported activity: 2024. Evidence: S1 printed p. 11.
Q1-Q3 · Crawl

I3 Wholesale credit operating model

Scope and capability: Approved workflow, control handoffs and exception owners for the selected North American credit process.

Dependency: No completed Crawl prerequisite. Engineering and process design proceed concurrently; integrated Q4 acceptance aligns the outputs.

Acceptance: Process owners approve the control inventory; 100% of pilot users pass scenario-based readiness assessment.

Reported activity: 2023. Evidence: S2 Our number one priority.

Walk rationale

Walk tests the foundations in bounded production settings before more entities or loans inherit their weaknesses. The reporting platform and ledger deployment test separate capabilities: report production and accounting reconciliation. The loan pilot tests workflow speed, controls and adoption for one North American product. Limited scope makes defects easier to correct before expansion. Run investment depends on repeated operating evidence, rather than software installation. Reporting expansion requires both reporting and ledger acceptance; regional loan migration requires a successful workflow pilot and a reconciled migration plan.

Q5-Q6 · Walk

I4 Regulatory reporting platform deployment

Scope and capability: One material report family in one legal entity, operated in parallel with existing production.

Dependency: I1 Data governance redesign, I2 Strategic data platform onboarding

Acceptance: Two on-time reporting cycles with zero unresolved material reconciliation breaks.

Reported activity: 2024. Evidence: S1 printed p. 11.
Q5-Q6 · Walk

I5 North America loan booking automation

Scope and capability: One committed corporate loan product and its amendments in North America.

Dependency: I1 Data governance redesign, I2 Strategic data platform onboarding, I3 Wholesale credit operating model

Acceptance: At least 50% lower median booking time for two months; at least 90% eligible adoption; no higher control-exception rate.

Reported activity: 2023-2024. Evidence: S2; S1 printed p. 12.
Q5-Q7 · Walk

I6 Unified ledger reporting deployment

Scope and capability: Parallel ledger reconciliation and accounting mappings in the same legal entity as I4.

Dependency: I1 Data governance redesign, I2 Strategic data platform onboarding

Acceptance: Two monthly closes with zero unexplained material differences; every scoped account mapped and accepted.

Reported activity: 2024. Evidence: S3 printed p. 76.

Run rationale

Run scales validated capabilities through controlled waves and embeds their recurring ownership. Reporting automation extends the accepted platform and ledger mappings across the agreed reporting inventory. Loan migration moves the scoped North American committed corporate portfolio onto strategic processing, using the proven workflow and controls. These are separate expansion deliverables, each with its own acceptance evidence. Teams must adopt shared certification, training and exception handling so the new process survives deployment. A failed gate pauses the affected wave; success on one path does not authorize scaling the other.

Q8-Q12 · Run

I7 Enterprise regulatory reporting automation

Scope and capability: Risk-ranked waves across the agreed material report families and entities. Finance and data owners adopt common certification, release calendars and exception routes.

Dependency: I4 Regulatory reporting platform deployment, I6 Unified ledger reporting deployment

Acceptance: 100% on-time scoped submissions; at least 95% workflow automation; no unresolved material breaks.

Reported activity: 2025 priority and progress. Evidence: S1 printed p. 11; S4 printed p. 78.
Q8-Q12 · Run

I8 North America wholesale loan platform migration

Scope and capability: Remaining scoped North American committed corporate loans. Credit and Operations adopt shared procedures, training and exception approvals; retire old processing after reconciliation.

Dependency: I5 North America loan booking automation

Acceptance: 100% scoped portfolio migrated and reconciled; at least 90% weekly eligible adoption; booking gains sustained without higher control exceptions.

Reported activity: 2025. Evidence: S4 printed p. 78.

I5 → I8 is our proposed release condition; the source does not prove this direct technical dependency. I4 and I6 can run concurrently; I7 requires both acceptance decisions.
